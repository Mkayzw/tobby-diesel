FAQ

Common questions

Can a burnt ECU really be repaired?

Often, yes. Many failures are damaged components on the board rather than the whole unit. We diagnose it first and tell you honestly whether it can be repaired.

Do I need to bring the whole machine?

For ECU repair and injector testing you can usually bring just the unit or injectors. For key coding and diagnostics we may need the machine — message us and we'll advise.

Which brands do you work on?

We specialise in Volvo ECMs (including 21358126) and work on a wide range of diesel trucks and heavy equipment. Send us the part number to confirm.

How do I get a price?

WhatsApp us the machine, part number and symptoms. Injector testing is $30; ECU repairs are quoted after diagnosis.
